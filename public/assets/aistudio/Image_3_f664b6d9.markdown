## Brand & Style

This design system blends soft neo-minimalism with joyful, tactile micro-interactions designed for mindful daily organization across habits, finances, and tasks. It targets intentional individuals seeking clarity, calm progression, and structured productivity without cold, clinical dashboard aesthetics. 

The aesthetic is built on three cornerstones:
- **Warm Editorial Calm:** Grounded in organic oat substrates, gentle bone borders, and deep charcoal typography that avoids harsh pure-black eye strain.
- **Vibrant Purposeful Signifiers:** Domain-specific chromatic coding gives each functional vertical (Habits, Expenses, Tasks, Streaks) immediate recognition and personality.
- **Physical Pillowy Depth:** Soft radii, tactile press-down states, and diffused elevation that make completing daily items feel tangible, rewarding, and light.

## Colors

The palette relies on warm, foundational neutrals punctuated by calibrated functional accents:

- **Foundational Surfaces:** Canvas backdrops utilize warm oat (`#FBF9F5` default, `#F5F2EB` muted secondary), framing crisp pure white (`#FFFFFF`) elevated cards with delicate boundary lines (`#EBE6DE`).
- **Typography & Structure:** Primary copy rests in Deep Ink (`#191C1E`), with high-emphasis titles in `#2D3134`. Secondary labels, metadata, and structural dividers use Warm Muted Slate (`#71777E`).
- **Domain Accents:**
  - *Habits:* Emerald Mint (`#10B981` base, `#059669` deep, `#E6F8F2` container wash).
  - *Expenses:* Coral Terracotta (`#F43F5E` base, `#E11D48` deep, `#FFE4E6` container wash).
  - *Tasks:* Electric Violet (`#6366F1` base, `#4F46E5` deep, `#EEF2FF` container wash).
  - *Streaks & Achievements:* Warm Amber Gold (`#F59E0B` base, `#FEF3C7` container wash).

## Typography

The type hierarchy is powered by **Plus Jakarta Sans**, chosen for its geometric precision softened by contemporary humanist touches. 

- **Display & Large Numerics:** Display and metric scales use tightened letter-spacing (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) and high weights (`700`, `800`) to present daily balances, completion percentages, and streak counts as bold, confident anchors.
- **Body & Legibility:** Standard body text is balanced with comfortable line-height ratios (`1.4` to `1.5`) for readable notes, logs, and subtasks.
- **Labels & Microcopy:** Category badges, statuses, and navigation labels adopt `600` and `700` weights with slight positive tracking (`+0.01em` to `+0.04em`) to maintain sharp legibility at compact dimensions.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is anchored in a continuous 4px base rhythm designed for compact handheld viewports:

- **Mobile Canvas Structure:** Screen boundaries maintain a strict outer margin of `16px` (`margin-mobile`), stepping up to `20px` (`margin`) on larger phablet/tablet dimensions. Content flows within a flexible single-column mobile stack and a 4-to-6 column fluid grid for tablets.
- **Card Padding & Cavities:** Standard container cards apply `space-lg` (`20px`) internal padding to isolate content comfortably from card perimeters. Dense list tiles drop to `space-md` (`12px`) vertically and `space-lg` horizontally.
- **Component Gaps:** Consecutive task rows, habit rings, and quick-add action bars separate with `space-sm` (`8px`) or `space-md` (`12px`) spacing tokens to preserve cohesive visual grouping without crowding.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth avoids cold drop shadows, relying instead on ambient, pillowy diffusion combined with structural borders:

- **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** Flat, matte oat surfaces (`#FBF9F5`) without shadows.
- **Level 1 (Default Cards & Tiles):** Pure white fill, framed by a crisp 1px structural stroke in `#EBE6DE`, coupled with an ultra-soft dual shadow:
  `box-shadow: 0 2px 4px rgba(25, 28, 30, 0.02), 0 8px 24px rgba(113, 119, 126, 0.06);`
- **Level 2 (Active Sheets & Modals):** Elevated bottom sheets and interactive pickers:
  `box-shadow: 0 4px 12px rgba(25, 28, 30, 0.03), 0 16px 36px rgba(25, 28, 30, 0.08);`
- **Floating Bar (Bottom Navigation):** Floating glass-tinted bar elevated with ambient warm shadow:
  `box-shadow: 0 8px 32px rgba(25, 28, 30, 0.08);` with a subtle top border `rgba(235, 230, 222, 0.8)`.
- **Interaction Press:** On tap/click, interactive cards scale slightly (`scale(0.985)`) with reduced shadow distance to simulate physical compression.

## Shapes

The design system enforces a pillowy, organic shape profile:

- **Base Elements & Badges:** Category indicators, chip filters, button controls, and status pills employ a full-round stadium/pill curvature (`rounded-full`).
- **Standard Cards:** Core module containers and summary widgets use `rounded-2xl` (`1.5rem` / `24px`) corner radii.
- **Hero Containers & Sheets:** Modal sheets, quick-log popovers, and hero dashboard metric pods feature `rounded-3xl` (`2rem` / `32px`) corners.
- **Checkers & Toggles:** Habit and task completion toggles are rendered as perfect, bouncy geometric circles (`50%` radius).

## Components

- **Buttons:**
  - *Primary:* Solid deep ink (`#191C1E`) background, crisp white text, full pill radius (`rounded-full`), `space-md` vertical and `space-xl` horizontal padding. Tactile bounce on press.
  - *Contextual Tints:* Domain-specific action buttons use full 100% tints (e.g., `#E6F8F2` for Habit actions, `#EEF2FF` for Task actions) paired with their deep color labels.
- **Habit Toggle Circles:**
  - 36px–44px circular click targets with a 2px inner border (`#EBE6DE`).
  - When checked, pops into a solid `#10B981` fill with an elastic scale bounce, revealing a crisp white checkmark icon.
- **Streak Badges:**
  - Warm amber container pill (`#FEF3C7`), housing a bold `#F59E0B` flame icon and numeric counter in `label-md` weight.
- **Cards (Habits, Expenses, Tasks):**
  - Crisp white substrate, 1px `#EBE6DE` perimeter border, `rounded-2xl` corners.
  - Left edge or indicator icon tinted with the functional color code (`#10B981` for habits, `#F43F5E` for expenses, `#6366F1` for tasks).
- **Expense Log Cells:**
  - Horizontal list cards showing merchant/category pill on the left, date subtitle in secondary text, and right-aligned bold monetary figures styled with `metric-num` at a scaled 18px.
- **Input Fields:**
  - Soft oat inset backgrounds (`#F5F2EB`) with invisible borders at rest.
  - Focused state transitions to white surface, 1.5px `#191C1E` boundary stroke, and subtle ambient ring.
- **Floating Bottom Tab Bar:**
  - Detached pill dock suspended 16px above the home indicator.
  - Translucent white background (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.92)` with `16px` backdrop-filter blur), housing icon-only tabs that transition into filled, colored micro-pills upon selection.
